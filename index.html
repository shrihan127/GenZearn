GenZearn is a tutor marketplace — not a course, not a guide, and not a generic tool.

We connect students who want 1:1 lessons with independent tutors who want paid bookings.

From tutor discovery to session checkout, the product is built to help students book and tutors earn in one place.

What You Are Buying on GenZearn

For students: access to a marketplace of tutors you can compare and book online.

For tutors: access to students looking to book paid 1:1 tutoring sessions.

For Students

Create a learner profile, choose a subject, compare tutor rates, and book 1:1 sessions in minutes.

For Tutors

Build your teaching profile, set your hourly rate, and get paid for every completed session.

Built-in Payments

At checkout, students pay online with one clear total before the lesson is confirmed.

How GenZearn Works

1) Student sends a session request. 2) Tutor accepts and confirms pricing. 3) Student pays online and receives lesson confirmation instantly.
