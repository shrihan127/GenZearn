Refund Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

1. Eligible Refund Windows

Students are eligible for a full refund when a booking is canceled at least 24 hours before the scheduled lesson start time.

2. Late Cancellations

Bookings canceled less than 24 hours before the lesson may be refunded partially, or issued as platform credit, based on tutor preparation and delivered materials.

3. Tutor No-Show or Tutor Cancellation

If a tutor cancels a confirmed booking or does not attend the lesson, students can choose either a full refund or a rebooking credit.

4. Technical Failures

If a verified platform issue prevents a session from taking place, GenZearn will review the issue and provide an appropriate refund or account credit.

5. How to Request a Refund

Submit refund requests through the Contact page within 5 days of the scheduled lesson date and include the booking confirmation details.

Our support team typically responds within 2 business days.

6. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds may be denied in cases of repeated abuse, policy violations, or attempts to bypass GenZearn billing and safety processes.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Continued use of GenZearn indicates acceptance of the current version.
