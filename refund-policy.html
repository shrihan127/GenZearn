Refund Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Payment Timing

All sessions are paid at the time of booking through GenZearn checkout.

2. Student Cancellation

If a student cancels at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time, they are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session may receive a partial refund or platform credit depending on tutor preparation time and lesson materials already shared.

3. Tutor Cancellation or No-Show

If a tutor cancels a confirmed session or does not join the session within a reasonable grace period, students are eligible for a full refund or may rebook with another tutor.

4. Technical Issues

If a verified platform issue prevents the session from taking place, GenZearn will review the case and issue a refund or credit when appropriate.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email the tutor within 5 days of the session date with your booking confirmation to receive a full refund from your tutor.

Our team typically responds within 2 business days.

6. Exceptions

Refund abuse, repeated late cancellations, or policy violations may limit eligibility for future refunds.

7. Questions

For any refund-related question, contact us through the Contact page.
