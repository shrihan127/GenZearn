Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking lessons, becoming a tutor, and payments on GenZearn.

What is GenZearn exactly?

GenZearn is a marketplace platform for tutors and students. We are not selling a standalone course, ebook, or one-time guide.

How do I book a lesson with a tutor?

Go to Find Tutors, choose a tutor that matches your subject, and submit a request with your preferred topic and hours. You will see the estimated total before confirming payment.

How do payments work?

Students pay online when confirming a session request. The estimated total is based on the tutor's hourly rate and the number of hours selected.

Can I sign up as both a student and tutor?

Yes. You can create an account and also apply to teach by completing the tutor profile form with your subjects and experience.

What subjects are available?

GenZearn supports a wide range of academic and skill-based subjects. Tutors list their specialties directly in their profiles.

How can tutors get verified?

Tutors provide qualification proof in their application. GenZearn reviews this information and updates verification status on the tutor profile.
