Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect account details (such as name, email, role, and subject preferences), booking and communication information, and basic technical usage data needed to operate GenZearn.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to provide tutor discovery, session scheduling, payments, account support, and platform safety features.

3. Student Privacy for U.S. Minors (FERPA/COPPA)

For users in the United States, GenZearn supports FERPA and COPPA compliance practices where applicable. This includes obtaining parent/guardian consent for children when required, limiting unnecessary collection of child data, and protecting education records shared through the platform.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to provide services (for example, with payment and infrastructure providers), comply with legal obligations, and maintain platform security.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain data only as long as needed for legitimate educational, operational, legal, and security purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may request updates or deletion of account information, subject to legal and contractual requirements. Parents or guardians may contact us about minor accounts and consent-related requests.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of GenZearn after updates means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact us via the Contact page.
