Privacy Policy

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, account profile details, tutoring preferences, and payment-related information needed to process bookings.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how users interact with GenZearn.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us run the platform (such as payment processors and hosting providers). We do not sell your personal information.

We keep personal data for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce platform policies.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may update account details through your profile settings. You can also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

GenZearn is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 was collected without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

9. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and post the revised policy on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
